👋 How this works

Three things you will do most:

  1. Home shows what needs your attention, what is coming up, and what you finished recently. Press Mark done when a report is sent.
  2. Reports that go out several times a month are in the Cycles tab – tick all the dates you finished and save once.
  3. Missing a report? Use Add report. It is recorded under your name, and you can fill it straight away.

Colours: green done on time · amber due today · red late. A report moves to its next date a few days before it is due again.
